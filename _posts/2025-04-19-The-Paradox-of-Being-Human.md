Humans are a contradiction. We have this incredible ability to reason, create, and understand the universe in ways no other species can. And yet, we still are just bunch of farms of neurotransmitters. But today, we are fully aware of that unlike the earlier times: the conscious awareness of being a machine — a self trapped in a biochemical system that still feels everything. I think the next step for humans today is that we are collectively agree that our biology is not sufficiently functional.





What happens when human agree that its organic form is a constraint? Human does everything due to its survival urges. Every single feature humans have can be associanted with their primitive, aggressive survival instincts. Therefore our biology should become a threat in order to agree on it as a constaint. Considerably, we have been improving our biology in various ways. Telescopes for better vision, bicycles for faster legs, drugs for a better immune system, and world wide web for a bigger brain. What have been leading for us to form all of that? Yes, survival urges for sure, but that may not be that straight forward considering today's technology. More apparent aspect could be curiosity. Which also is a survival tool; information seeking <=> better decision making. 






Should AI implement survival urges?
