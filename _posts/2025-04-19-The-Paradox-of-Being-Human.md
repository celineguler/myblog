Humans are a contradiction. We have this incredible ability to reason, create, and understand the universe in ways no other species can. And yet, we still are just bunch of farms of neurotransmitters. But today, we gained more awareness of that unlike the earlier times: the conscious awareness of being a machine — a self trapped in a biochemical system that still feels (or collectively transports neurotransmitters) everything. I think the next step for humans today is that we agree that our biology is not sufficiently functional and we need to improve that.


j


What happens when human agree that its organic form is a constraint? Human does every practical thing due to its survival urges. Every single feature human can be associanted with their primitive, aggressive survival instincts. Therefore our biology should become a threat in order to agree on it as a constaint. Considerably, we have been improving our biology in various ways. Telescopes for better vision, bicycles for faster legs, drugs for a better immune system, and world wide web for a bigger brain. What have been leading for us to form all of that? Yes, survival urges for sure, but that may not be that straight forward considering the primitive tool 2.0 (today's technology). More apparent aspect could be curiosity.

#### __Should AI implement survival urges?__


How could even that work out? A machine does something because it is programmed to do so. We do things because we lean to survive. Would any other way would work out, if humans weren’t programmed with survival instincts? For sustainability?

The answer is no. Our motivation to do things is to survive. Human brain can function optimally under relevant circumstances when danger occurs. It can combine multiple aspects and foresee the outcome, _in order to survive_. But of course, in the length of what an organic organism can manage. Now, how about machines? Survival is a good driver for biological organisms but how about machines?

We have a great potential to function silicon where it's only constraint is the physics. That's called AI.
