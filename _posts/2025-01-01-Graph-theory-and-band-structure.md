title: Graph theory and band structure

---

As of 2025, we cannot solve Schrödinger Equations for more than a few electrons. If your system includes less than 10-20 electrons, you are welcome to solve SE. If you have more than 10-20 electrons, in this intermediate state you basically can't do anything. And when you're up to more than 1000-2000 electrons, we have approximations such as DFT, tight-binding, Hartree-Fock etc. which helps you form a hamiltonian for your system. Solving SE with this corresponding hamiltonian and its eigenvalues will be obtained which is called the electronic structure.
